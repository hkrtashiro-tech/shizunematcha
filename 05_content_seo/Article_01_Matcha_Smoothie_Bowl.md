# Matcha Smoothie Bowl for a Mindful Morning

A smoothie bowl is one of the simplest ways to start your day with color, nourishment, and calm. This matcha version brings together creamy banana, gentle matcha energy, and your favorite toppings — all in one beautiful bowl.

No blender tricks, no long prep. Just a quiet, nourishing start to your morning.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 tsp matcha powder (about 2g)
- 1 frozen banana (sliced before freezing)
- 1/2 cup / 120 ml milk (oat, almond, or cow's milk)
- 1/4 cup / 60 g plain Greek yogurt
- 1 tsp honey or maple syrup (optional)

### Suggested Toppings

- Fresh berries (blueberries, strawberries, or raspberries)
- Granola or muesli
- Sliced banana
- Chia seeds or hemp seeds
- Coconut flakes
- A light dusting of matcha powder

---

## Steps

### Step 1

Sift 1 tsp of matcha powder into a small cup. Add 1 tablespoon of warm water and stir until smooth. This prevents any lumps in your bowl.

### Step 2

Add the frozen banana, milk, yogurt, and the matcha paste into a blender.

### Step 3

Blend until thick and creamy. The texture should be thicker than a regular smoothie — more like soft-serve. If it's too thick, add a splash of milk. If too thin, add a few ice cubes.

### Step 4

Pour the mixture into a bowl. Arrange your favorite toppings on top.

### Step 5

Take a moment before you eat. Notice the colors. The green of the matcha, the deep blue of the berries, the warmth of the granola. Then enjoy — slowly.

---

## Tips

- **Frozen banana is key.** It gives the bowl its thick, creamy texture without ice cream. Peel and slice bananas the night before, then freeze in a bag.
- **Adjust sweetness to your taste.** Ripe bananas often provide enough natural sweetness. Taste the blend before adding honey or syrup.
- **For a bolder matcha flavor**, increase to 1.5 tsp (about 3g) of matcha powder.
- **Make it plant-based.** Swap Greek yogurt for coconut yogurt and use oat or almond milk for a fully vegan bowl.
- Shizune Matcha's smooth, balanced flavor works especially well here — the natural umami pairs beautifully with the sweetness of fruit and granola.

---

## Why We Love It

A smoothie bowl isn't just breakfast. It's a small act of care — choosing ingredients with intention, arranging them with attention, and enjoying them without rushing.

Paired with matcha, it becomes something a little more grounding. A calm, colorful start before the day begins.
