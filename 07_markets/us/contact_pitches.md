# Email 1 — PRISM Logistics

Subject: NOP Handler Partnership Inquiry — Japanese Organic Matcha Brand (Tens–Hundreds kg/year)

To: PRISM Logistics（公式問い合わせフォーム / 営業窓口経由）

---

Dear PRISM Logistics Team,

I'm Hikaru Tashiro, Brand Owner of SHIZUNE MATCHA (https://shizunematcha.com/), a premium Japanese matcha brand operated by Traverse Asia. We sell Shizune Matcha on Amazon US with our conventional product line, and we're now preparing to launch our USDA Organic / JAS organic matcha in the US market.

What we're looking for:
- A US partner with NOP Handler certification to serve as Importer of Record for our incoming shipments
- Either of the following operational models works for us — please advise which fits PRISM:
  - Model A: PRISM acts as IOR / Handler-of-record only; our existing Japanese forwarder (which has a US entity but is not NOP-certified) handles physical logistics
  - Model B: PRISM provides the full chain — IOR, receiving, NOP-compliant storage, FBA prep, and forwarding to Amazon FBA centers

Our basics:
- Product: Finished retail goods — 40g tins and 80g pouches
- Annual volume: Tens to a few hundred kilograms (small lot)
- Origin: JAS-certified organic farms in Japan; NOPID issuance is already arranged on the Japanese side
- Destination: Primarily Amazon FBA in the US

A few quick questions to start:
1. Does PRISM currently hold an active NOP Handler certification?
2. Can PRISM act as Importer of Record for organic shipments from Japan?
3. Which of the two models above can PRISM accommodate?
4. Is our annual volume range (tens–hundreds of kg) workable for you?

I'd appreciate the chance to discuss further. Looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,

Hikaru Tashiro
